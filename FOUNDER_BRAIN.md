# Founder Brain — LetsVibeAI

The living context file for the LetsVibeAI content engine. Anyone (human or AI
agent) writing, packaging or reviewing content for the brand reads this first.
Update it every week from the founder call, business stories and what
performed. Inspired by the "Head of Content" operating model: one owner for
positioning, extraction, packaging and optimization.

Internal working document — not published copy.

---

## 1. The Business

- **Brand:** LetsVibeAI — The AI Skills Institution (letsvibeai.com)
- **Promise:** Learn AI. Build with AI. Grow with AI.
- **What it is:** free, plain-English, architecture-first courses that take
  people from "I've never coded" to shipping real AI tools, automations and
  web apps.
- **Offers (current on site):**
  - Free 10-module Vibe Coding curriculum (the flagship; "core course")
  - Live Build Nights — $49/session (Chicago or online)
  - 1:1 AI Build Coaching — $149/hour
  - Team & Workforce Workshops — $1,500/half-day
- **Contact:** hello@letsvibeai.com

## 2. The Person (voice & story)

- **Founder:** Patrick Diamitani, Chicago, IL. Founder & Lead Instructor.
- **Background (his own bio):** learned AI from practical execution; leads GTM
  AI & automation at scale; has built 150+ custom AI workflows and
  applications. Also founded Artispreneur (music-business platform) and
  SalesGency (GTM automation).
- **Voice rules:**
  - Plain English. Clear before clever.
  - One real-world analogy per idea ("film director, not camera operator").
  - Production over demos: real code, real databases, real payments.
  - Never invent numbers, testimonials or results. If a claim can't be shown,
    it doesn't ship.
- **Recurring frames:** "direction beats guessing"; "every app has an
  architecture"; typist → director.

## 3. ICP & Positioning

- **Who it's for:** creators, founders, operators, marketers, educators and
  career changers who want to build real software with AI without a CS
  background.
- **What to be known for:** the architecture-first way to direct AI agents —
  the school for people who want to *ship*, not just chat.
- **Topics to own:** vibe coding method, agent harnesses & skills, AI news
  translated for operators, free-tool stacks (GitHub + Vercel + Supabase),
  GTM automation.
- **Topics to avoid:** hype-only AI news, get-rich angles, unverified income
  claims.

## 4. The Material (content pillars)

1. **The Daily Vibe** — daily AI briefing: what moved, one tool, one build
   idea. (Newsletter + site blog; LiveBuildAI archive is the back catalog.)
2. **Curriculum lessons** — modules from the free course, repurposed.
3. **Build logs** — real builds (Artispreneur, Catana, courses) told as
   architecture-first stories.
4. **Tool guides** — practical setups (e.g. GitHub + AI keys; Vercel/Supabase
   free stack).

## 5. The History

  with builder implications. Free curriculum as the top-of-funnel anchor.
- **Watch:** blog once carried placeholder posts with stock images — killed
  Oct 2026. Real posts only, always.
- **Open decisions (Patrick):** free-at-launch vs charge from day one;
  Beehiiv vs Substack for the newsletter.

## 6. The Weekly Loop

1. **Pull the data** — which posts/videos got reads, saves, replies, signups.
2. **Scan the market** — what the ICP is talking about this week.
3. **Founder interview (45 min)** — extract stories, decisions, opinions;
   append to sections 2–5 above.
4. **Build the week** — assign every piece a funnel job (discover → trust →
   act), write, review against voice rules, schedule.

## 7. Scoreboard

Track authority, not vanity: Seen → Recognized (they know what Pat talks
about) → Associated (they link ideas to him) → Referenced (they quote and
bring him up) → Contacted (DMs, referrals, booked calls). Review monthly.
