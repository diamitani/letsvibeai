## What This Guide Covers

How to get a free year of Perplexity Pro ($200 value) including Comet browser beta access, through a PayPal/Venmo promotional offer.

---

## The Offer

PayPal and Venmo US users can activate Perplexity Pro at no cost for 12 months. This includes:

- Unlimited Pro Search with GPT-4, Claude, Grok, and other frontier models
- Comet browser beta — AI assistant with cross-tab context
- File upload and code/document analysis
- Ad-free experience, no model training from user data

At the end of 12 months, billing kicks in at the then-current rate. Cancel anytime before the renewal date.

---

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Set Up PayPal

If you don't have a PayPal account:
1. Visit paypal.com
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Choose "Personal" account
4. Enter email, verify phone
5. Link a bank account or debit card

### Step 2: Find the Perplexity Offer

1. Open the PayPal mobile app
2. Navigate to "Offers" or "Subscriptions"
3. Look for the Perplexity Pro banner (may be under "Promotions" or "Partner Deals")
4. Tap to start the sign-up flow

### Step 3: Activate Perplexity Pro

1. You'll be prompted to create a new Perplexity account or log into an existing one
2. The account must never have had Pro before
3. When asked for payment method, select PayPal
4. No upfront charge during the promotional period

### Step 4: Verify Activation

1. Go to Perplexity settings → "Plan" tab
2. You should see: "Perplexity Pro – Trial (12 months)"
3. Start using Pro Search immediately
