## What a project holds

1. **Knowledge and working files**
2. **Instructions** just for this work stream
3. **Memory** of the decisions you've made
4. **Scheduled tasks**, like a weekly report
5. **Conversation history**

## One project per work stream

Don't dump everything into one giant bucket. Make one project for each stream of work, such as **a launch**, **sales enablement** or **weekly reporting**.

## Set up a project in three moves

1. **Add your key files first:** examples, templates and past work.
2. **Write the project instructions:** audience, standards and what to avoid.
3. **Then ask.** Claude starts already briefed.

## New-hire power move

Make a project for your new role and add your onboarding docs. Then ask Claude to:

- explain the jargon,
- map the key people, and
- draft your 30-day plan.

## Recap

A project keeps files, instructions, memory and schedules together, so every chat starts briefed.
