## Two ways to put work on a schedule

| | Where it runs | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Routines** | Anthropic's cloud, even with your laptop closed | Work that must run no matter what |
| **Scheduled tasks** | Your computer, through the Claude app | Work that uses your local files and apps |

Type **/schedule** or use the **Scheduled** sidebar, and set it up in plain English:

> *"Every Monday at 8, build a competitive brief and save it to this project."*

## Four to start with

- A **weekly competitor roundup**
- A **daily pipeline snapshot**
- A **monthly campaign rollup**
- **Meeting prep** built from your calendar every morning

## Make it stick

1. Pick **one job** you repeat.
2. Ask for it in **plain English**.
3. **Review the first run** closely.
4. Tweak it, then let it run.

## Recap

Routines and scheduled tasks turn your weekly chores into work that runs itself.

> **Your mission:** schedule one weekly task that saves you at least an hour.
