Tone of Voice — 3 Pillars
The Atlas voice is constant. Tone flexes with context.
🎯 Authoritative
We've explored every corner of global expansion — so we speak with real authority. Confident but never pushy. Smart but never sassy. Knowledgeable, substantiated, definitive.
"When you hire in Brazil, statutory benefits aren't optional — they're the cost of doing business right. Here's what you need to know."
🌐 Inclusive
Our language empowers decision-makers at every level. We never condescend to experts or alienate newcomers. Culturally neutral. Cross-functional.
"Whether you're hiring your first international employee or managing a team across 30 countries, the principles are the same: compliance first, people always."
🎯 Measured
Clear-sighted, focused, purposeful. We speak as if addressing a single professional at a specific point in their journey. Never scatter-shot.
"Your next hire in Germany requires a works council notification. Here's the exact process, so there are no surprises."
What Atlas is NEVER
Avoid these tones in all content
🚫 Weak
❌ "We could potentially help with some of your global hiring needs, depending on your situation."
✅ "We handle global hiring in 160+ countries — compliantly, directly, and fast."
🚫 Exclusive / Jargon-heavy
❌ "Leveraging our best-in-class synergies across our vertically-integrated EOR ecosystem..."
✅ "Our global team handles the legal and compliance complexity — so you can focus on the hire."
🚫 Careless / Loud
❌ "WE ARE THE BEST IN GLOBAL HIRING!! 🔥🔥🔥 DON'T MISS OUT!!!"
✅ "160+ countries. One partner. Zero shortcuts."
Atlas Vocabulary
Preferred terms — always use these, not the alternatives
✅ Use This❌ Not ThisWhy It Matters
Employer of Record (EOR)PEO, staffing agencyEOR is the correct category — PEO is a different service model
Atlas HXM or Atlas"we" on first mentionAlways name the brand on first reference in any piece
Global talent managementHR outsourcingToo generic — undersells the scope of what Atlas provides
Direct EOR modelThird-party modelHighlights Atlas's key differentiator: no intermediaries
Hire in [country]Expand to [country]Hiring is the specific action — expansion is too vague
HXM Platform"our software" / "the tool"Always use the product name — it's a named product
160+ countries"global" (alone)Specificity builds credibility — never say global without a number
Compliance"legal stuff"Compliance is serious — casual language undercuts trust

Typography
Gelion is the required typeface. Never default to Poppins unless a platform genuinely blocks custom fonts.
Type Hierarchy
Role Font Size Leading
HeadlineGelion SemiBold80pt86pt / 105%
SubheadGelion Medium30pt37pt / 110%
BodyGelion Regular18pt24pt / 110%
Kerning: -3 on all roles. Avoid ligatures. Never mix alignment styles in the same layout.
Gelion Weights Available
Thin ExtraLight Light Regular ★ Body Medium ★ Subhead SemiBold ★ Headline Bold ExtraBold Black
Poppins is a technical fallback only — for platforms that genuinely block custom font files. Never use Poppins as a default. Always use Gelion.
