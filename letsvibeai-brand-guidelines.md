# LetsVibeAI Brand System v1.0
**Position:** The AI skills institution for work and life.

## Brand idea
**Learn. Build. Grow.**
LetsVibeAI makes AI understandable, practical and actionable for beginners, career changers, creators, founders, operators, educators, workforce programs and public-service teams.

The identity is built around a **Gateway V**:
- **V** = Vibe, Venture, Velocity, upward movement.
- **Central signal** = AI + the learner/person at the center.
- **Open geometry** = a gateway into capability, not a closed technical system.
- **Three arcs** = learning → application → amplification.

## Logo
Primary mark: geometric Gateway V + central signal.
Primary wordmark: LetsVibeAI.
Watermark: symbol only at 4–8% opacity.
Do not distort, rotate, outline, add shadows, recolor outside the approved palette, or place over busy photography.

## Palette
- Deep Navy #071B3A — trust, stability, institutional credibility
- Signal Blue #2F80ED — clarity, technology, action
- Growth Green #34D399 — progress, opportunity, confidence
- Learning Violet #7C5CFC — creativity, possibility, discovery
- Signal Cyan #20C7D9 — energy, connection
- Mist #F4F7FB — clean learning surfaces
- Ink #10213F — primary text

## Typography
Primary: Sora (Bold/Semibold/Medium/Regular).
Fallback: Inter, Arial, sans-serif.
Headlines: Sora Bold.
Subheads: Sora Semibold.
Body: Sora Regular.
Use generous line-height and short paragraphs.

## Voice
Clear before clever. Encouraging without being childish. Practical, confident, human.
Say: "Here's how it works."
Avoid: jargon for its own sake, fear-based AI messaging, hype claims.

## Messaging architecture
Master promise: **Learn AI. Build with AI. Grow with AI.**
Institutional descriptor: **The AI Skills Institution.**
Longer positioning: **LetsVibeAI teaches people how to use AI confidently in their jobs and lives—then turns learning into real projects, workshops and workforce/community outcomes.**

## Design principles
1. Teach, don't intimidate.
2. Show the path.
3. Put people before technology.
4. Make complexity legible.
5. Celebrate progress.
6. Build for access: strong contrast, readable type, keyboard-friendly UI.
7. Prefer diagrams, checklists, examples and real work over decorative AI imagery.

## Signature graphic language
Use V-shaped frames, upward pathways, signal arcs, nodes, checklists and architectural diagrams.
Never use generic robot heads as the primary brand metaphor.

## Product architecture
Consumer learning → projects → workshops → education → workforce → city/community services.
The same visual system should scale from a free lesson to an institutional program.

## Trademark / legal note
This document defines a proposed brand direction, not a legal clearance opinion. Before public rollout, perform a professional trademark search for **LetsVibeAI**, the Gateway V symbol, and key slogans in the intended classes/jurisdictions; then file where appropriate. Reserve domain/social handles and document first commercial use.
