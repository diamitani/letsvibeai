## Claude works three ways

- **Chat:** think and draft.
- **Agentic tasks (Cowork):** get multi-step work done on your files and apps.
- **Claude Code:** build software.

## How a Cowork task runs

1. **You describe the outcome** you want.
2. **Claude makes a plan** and breaks it into steps.
3. **It works through your files and apps.** You can steer it at any time.
4. **It hands back finished work.**

## The big difference

| Chat | Cowork |
| --- | --- |
| Gives you text to copy, paste and format yourself | Gives you the finished file, built from your real material |

## Recap

Cowork turns Claude from a chatbot into a teammate that finishes real work.
