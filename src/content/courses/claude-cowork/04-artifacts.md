## What an artifact can be

- **Docs** you can edit together and export to Word or PDF
- **Slide decks** built from your content that export to PowerPoint
- **Designs** like one-pagers and landing pages
- **Dashboards, calculators and small tools**

## Ask well, get better work

The quality of the output reflects the quality of the ask:

- Name **the deliverable** and how big it is.
- Name **the audience**.
- Say **what to cover**, in your brand's voice.

## The artifact loop

1. **Ask** for the artifact.
2. **Review it like an editor.**
3. **Highlight any part** and ask Claude to change just that spot.
4. **Share or export** it.

## Good to know

- Artifacts start **private**. You choose when to share.
- People you share with sign in to view.
- They're great for **prototypes**, not production software.
