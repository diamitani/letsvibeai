## Why connectors

Pasting data into a chat goes stale the moment you do it. A connector lets Claude **read live data** and **take real actions**, with your approval.

## Point-and-click connectors

- **Email and calendar**
- **Chat tools** like Slack
- **Drives and documents**
- **Project tools** like Asana or Jira

Then Claude can prep your meetings, summarize threads and update your trackers.

## Three ways Claude reaches your tools

| Way | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Connectors** | Point-and-click integrations |
| **MCP servers** | The open standard, for databases and internal tools |
| **Plugins** | Bundles of connectors and skills (next lesson) |

## Stay safe

- Check what each connector can read.
- Start with **read-only** access where you can.
- **Approve anything** that sends, posts or deletes.
