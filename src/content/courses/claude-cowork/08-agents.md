## How Claude handles big jobs

For big jobs, Claude **makes a plan and splits the work**. Subagents take pieces in parallel:

- one **researches**,
- one **drafts**,
- one **checks**.

Each works in its own clean space and reports back.

## Agents reach beyond your folders

- **Claude in Chrome** works inside your browser, filling forms and clicking through sites.
- **Computer use** lets Claude operate desktop apps, with your permission.
- **Claude for Microsoft 365** works in Word, Excel and Outlook.

## Write a good brief

Delegating well means telling Claude:

1. **The goal** and why it matters
2. **What done looks like**
3. **What not to touch**
4. **What to report back**

You don't have to watch every step. Review the plan, steer when it drifts, and **approve anything risky**.

## Recap

Agents split big work, reach your browser and apps, and report back. You lead.

> **Your mission:** delegate one multi-step task end to end: research, draft and a finished file.
