## Work with Claude like a trusted outside consultant

- **Keep out** personal data, passwords and confidential strategy, unless your company approves it.
- **Always review before you share:** check facts, brand and sensitive data.

> **Rule of thumb:** if you wouldn't email it to an outside consultant, don't paste it into Claude.

## Climb the ladder

**Chat → Projects → Skills → Scheduled tasks → Agents.** Each rung makes the next one easier.

## Turn it into a job or a promotion

1. **Build a portfolio** of three real before-and-after projects.
2. **Share a skill you wrote** to show how you think.
3. **Put a number on it**, like hours saved each week.
4. **Explain how you review the work.** That's the judgment employers want.

## Recap

Stay safe, climb the ladder, and turn what you build into proof.

> **Your mission:** automate one real task, then write a one-paragraph case study with the hours saved.

That's Claude Cowork. You've got the whole system. Now go put it to work and get credit for it.
