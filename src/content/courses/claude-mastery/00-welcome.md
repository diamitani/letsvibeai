> **Most people use AI like a search box. The best builders direct it like a team.**

Describe the software you want in plain language, typed or spoken, and AI agents write the code. Your job moves from *typist* to *director*.

## What's inside

- **Eight core lessons:** set up your harness, write context files that remember, write prompts that survive production, package your expertise as skills, connect your real tools, build commands and workflows, lead a team of agents, and roll it out safely to a team.
- **Two masterclasses:** *One Must Act*, a theory of goal achievement, and *Claude in October 2026*, a tour of everything Claude can do today.
- **A hands-on mission at the end of every lesson,** so you finish each video with something real.

## How to take this course

1. Watch one lesson a day. Each one is about two minutes.
2. Do the mission while it's fresh. Open Claude Code (or your harness) next to the video.
3. Tick **Mark complete** so your progress is saved on this device.

The LetsVibeAI Standard: Built by operators for builders. Learn, build, ship.
