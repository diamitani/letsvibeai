## Every goal is multiplication.

One Must Act says any goal is reached by multiplication. You start at zero. You build a whole, which is one. Then you multiply it with the right people, agents and systems, until the result, measured in reality, reaches one hundred.

- **0 · The start** — Where you really are today
- **1 · The whole** — Complete and working, end to end
- **2–99 · Multipliers** — People, agents and systems
- **100 · The goal** — Specific, measured in reality

## Finish the one before you multiply.

Here's the catch. Multiplication only works on a whole. A fraction shortchanges every multiplier you add, and the gap widens as you scale. Point nine nine times two is one point nine eight, not two. So finish the one first.

**A 99% foundation** vs **A whole foundation**

| A fraction | A whole |
| --- | --- |
| 0.99 × 2 = 1.98 | 1 × 2 = 2 |
| 0.70 × 100 = 70 | 1 × 100 = 100 |
| Every multiplier is shortchanged | Every multiplier pays in full |

## Four questions. Every goal.

Every goal has four quantities. One hundred, the goal. What exactly do you want, and by when? One, the whole. What must exist, working end to end? Two to ninety nine, the multipliers. Who carries which share? And zero, the start. Where are you, honestly, today?

- **100 · The goal** — What exactly, and by when?
- **1 · The whole** — What must exist, working end to end?
- **2–99 · Multipliers** — Who carries which share?
- **0 · The start** — Where are you, honestly, today?

## Define from the end. Build from the start.

Define 100 → 1 → 2–99 → 0  ·  Build 0 → 1 → 2–99 → 100

The order matters. Define from the end: the goal, the whole, the multipliers, then the start. Then build from the start: zero, to one, to your multipliers, to one hundred.

## Finish. Process. Effective.

Every step is held to Pat's standard, called FPE. Finish. Always ship complete things. No placeholders. Process. Work from repeatable systems, like skills and checklists. Effective. Judge it by results in reality, not by effort.

- **Finish** — Ship complete things — no placeholders
- **Process** — Repeatable systems, skills, checklists
- **Effective** — Judged by results, not effort

## What you just learned.

- 0 → 1 → 2–99 → 100
- Finish the whole before you multiply
- Hold every step to FPE
