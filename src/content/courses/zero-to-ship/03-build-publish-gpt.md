## Combine the Assets

With the system instructions from Day 2 and the knowledge sources from today, you can now build your GPT.

### Build Checklist

1. Open the GPT Builder and go to the "Configure" tab
2. **Name and Description**: Fill in the AI Content Writer name and description
3. **Instructions**: Paste the detailed system instructions you generated
4. **Conversation Starters**: Add helpful prompts like "Write a blog post about..."
5. **Knowledge**: Upload files based on your knowledge base research
6. **Capabilities**: Enable Web Browsing and DALL-E Image Generation
7. **Test in the Preview Pane**: Interact with your GPT to see if it follows instructions
8. **Iterate and Refine**: Tweak instructions based on test results
9. **Save and Publish**: Click "Save" and choose your publishing option

### Practice Prompt: Knowledge Base Architect

> I am building an "AI Content Writer" GPT. Create a list of 5 high-quality knowledge sources for copywriting, SEO, and social media content. For each: Title, Source Type, Description, URL, and why it is valuable. Format as a markdown table.

🎉 **Congratulations!** You have successfully designed and built your first Custom GPT.
