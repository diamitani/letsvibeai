## Why Automation Matters for AI

Your AI assistants are powerful, but they are most effective when connected to the other tools you use every day. Automation is the digital glue that makes this possible.

### What Automation Enables

- **Connect AI to Your World**: Feed data from forms, emails, or CRMs into your AI, and send AI-generated output to documents, databases, or marketing platforms
- **Scale Your AI**: Go from processing one task at a time to processing thousands automatically
- **Create End-to-End Solutions**: Chain multiple services together (e.g., new email → GPT drafts reply → saves to Google Doc for review)
